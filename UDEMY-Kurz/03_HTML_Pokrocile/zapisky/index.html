    • Chicken

    The chicken (Gallus gallus domesticus) is a large and round short-winged bird, domesticated from the red junglefowl of Southeast Asia around 8,000 years ago. Most chickens are raised for food, providing meat and Eggs; others are kept as pets[1] or for cockfighting.

    Chickens are common and widespread domestic animals, with a total population of 26.5 billion as of 2023, and an annual [2] production of more than 50 billion birds. A hen bred for laying can produce over 300 eggs per year. There are numerous cultural references to chickens in folklore, religion, and literature.
